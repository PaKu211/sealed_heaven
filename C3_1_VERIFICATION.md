# C3.1 — Martial Arts & Mentorship (revision 21)

Source baseline is `d9b659e`, not the `0b9659e` hash in the request. `docs/GDD_v0.2.md` recommends a compact, authored 2–3-technique prototype rather than procedural empty terrain. C3.1 keeps the existing map and frozen C0.1 kernel. Default ASCII writes `checkpoint.c31`; `--c30` retains revision 20. No push, merge or human playtest.

Two named, living and available teachers offer different persistent forms through `drill <teacher>`: Ren Zhi's Reed Palm prepares one parry after a two-damage strike; Stonebank's envoy teaches a three-damage Stone Fist that incurs greater physical strain. Two face-to-face lessons cost one **real coin each**, transferred to the relevant sect treasury; switching teachers resets partial lessons. Membership remains optional, but incompatible sect allegiance blocks hostile teaching. `wait` recovers player strain. Technique use and NPC retaliation share the same actor-owned form and strain state; wounded, fighting, exhausted or absent teachers cannot teach. A Hall deputy learns Reed Palm autonomously after observing its mentor, can use its parry in combat, and does not learn if the teacher dies before teaching. Overstrained NPCs recover rather than losing their ability forever. `martial` shows personal state; `martiallog` displays only lessons the player witnessed.

Acceptance tests use public turn commands from seed zero: an independent player walks the road, pays for two Stone lessons and lands a three-damage `technique` where an ordinary `attack` deals two. A separate route learns Reed Palm and tests its guarded response. A passive branch shows the deputy learning without the player; NPC lesson receipts are not leaked to the player. Fixed-custody tests verify the exact two-coin transfer, dead-teacher interruption and trained NPC parry. Four seeds run 180 turns with checkpoint replay at multiple intervals, monotonic lesson identities and finite lot count. Legacy revision 20 restores without martial state. Actual headless binary route: `/tmp/opencode/c31-ascii-play.txt`.

Offline Linux gates: root `cargo test --offline` **297 passed, 1 ignored**, 2 doctests passed; playable `--no-default-features` **58 library tests** plus all integration suites passed. Root and playable strict all-target Clippy (`-D warnings`), Rustfmt checks and whitespace checks clean. Performance: four seeded 180-turn/replay sequences completed in under one second in a focused test; no formal release-build or laptop benchmark. Windows and human playtesting unverified.

Still an alpha slice, not C4.0: only the deputy practices independently; Qi/meridians, injury, independent teachers, professions and death legacy are not yet systemic. Next priority is C3.2: physically grounded Qi and meridian condition that can interrupt technique use and demand recovery, applying to player **and NPCs** without global XP or free buffs.
