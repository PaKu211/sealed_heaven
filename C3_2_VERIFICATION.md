# C3.2 — Qi circulation and meridian condition (revision 22)

From C3.1 `280e709`. Default ASCII writes `checkpoint.c32`; `--c31` and all older rules keep their own behavior and digests. Frozen C0.1 kernel untouched.

`drawqi` requires a carried physical herb and proximity to Qinghe shrine or Reed Hall; it consumes exactly one herb into the existing consumption ledger and restores one bodily Qi (maximum three). `circulate` spends Qi to open one of two meridian channels; training strain or serious injury diverts the effort into persistent blockage instead. Two blocked channels prevent further drawing, circulation, and martial techniques until `mend` at a staffed clinic consumes real shrine medicine. Repeating a completed circulation is rejected before spending Qi. Under revision 22, a learned player or NPC technique requires open channels **and** available Qi, and spends Qi when committed. Earlier martial revisions retain their previous costs.

The deputy learns Reed Palm independently as in C3.1, then draws Qi from actual Reed Hall offerings, circulates it, and can use the same technique rules as the player. Masters start with authored bodily training and finite Qi; running out forces ordinary attacks. NPC need selection is local and stable: injury/care, available offerings, physical travel, exhaustion and rival obligations may interrupt progress. Meridian receipts are monotonic, included in the revision-22 digest, and `meridianlog` reveals only player-witnessed events.

Public simulation and ASCII routes gather three real herbs on the road, walk to the shrine, draw and circulate twice, then retain one Qi with two open channels. A complementary adversarial test injects *only* high strain to isolate unsafe circulation, then uses public turns for obstruction, physical travel to the clinic and medicine-consuming care; this fixture is **not** save/replay evidence. Separate tests prove technique denial without channels or Qi, expenditure on an actual strike, and inherited spirit-ore ward behavior. Four independent seeds run 180 autonomous turns; full regional herb material is checked every turn against actual regrowth, with save/replay and event-ID checks at the end. The unmodified binary command transcript is `/tmp/opencode/c32-ascii-play.txt`; no human playtest.

Offline Linux gates: root `cargo test --offline`: **301 passed, 1 ignored**, 2 doctests passed. Playable `--no-default-features`: **59 library tests** plus all integration suites passed. Both strict all-target Clippy runs (`-D warnings`), Rustfmt and whitespace checks passed. No Windows verification or performance claims.

Remaining: meridian care currently follows the existing clinic staffing model; there is no broad injury anatomy, tribulation, spiritual environment quality or inheritance system. C3.3 should make wounds, retreat and recovery tactical for both player and NPC rather than expanding the same herb courier case.
