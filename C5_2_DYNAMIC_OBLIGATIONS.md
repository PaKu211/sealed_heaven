# C5.2 - Dynamic obligations and social consequences

Baseline C5.1 bde7302ae7ff9ea19161bbeee068b05b70a2ca93. Rules revision 39; isolated checkpoint.c52. No new dependencies, authored lore or automatic migration.

## Commitments
Every coordinated freight dispatch opens exactly one material promise, using the existing team identity. C5.1 remains authoritative for actual goods, role assignments, local demand, delivery and cancellation. An active, healthy carrier with intact cargo has a low-priority delivery commitment (score 49); medical, combat, rest and institutional priorities remain superior. The exact scheduler excludes this exceptional decision from the routine certificate. No new inventory, payment, teleportation or unrestricted goal override.

The actual delivery handler or existing project cancellation produces one resolution: Fulfilled, ChangedCargo, CarrierLost, Released (existing voluntary companion withdrawal) or Expired. Resolution is not proof that a distant partner knows what happened.

## Knowledge and social consequences
The living contributor learns the outcome only when the carrier reports face-to-face within reach and line of sight, outside immediate retreat/retaliation and with nonhostile relations, or when the contributor locally reads a matching successful manifest at the physical destination. Acknowledged fulfillment adds at most one directional regard, without changing hostility, gratitude debt, or funds. Release decreases existing positive regard by at most one, never below neutral, and delays another coordinated promise for 96 ticks. Changed cargo and expired promises count as disruptions/missed delivery but never imply culpability or reduce regard. Carrier death cannot produce testimony; an already posted fulfillment manifest may still be read if its carrier subsequently dies. Unreported outcomes expire after 480 ticks, with no social penalty.

One pending notice per carrier; ordinary trade and travel are unaffected. New paired dispatches wait until the prior notice is acknowledged or expires. Acknowledged reliability is pair-specific and does not become global reputation. No accusation of theft or intentional betrayal from inventory differences. Historical counters, bounded history (128), public witnessed journal (48), pending notices, and retry intervals are included in canonical state. Earlier checkpoint revisions retain their behavior. Existing team journal supplies known shipment manifests; the promise journal shows only witnessed verbal acknowledgement, not private reactions or remote tracking.

## Scope
This is a conservative freight commitment foundation, not generalized promises among all actors, autonomous malicious betrayal, faction diplomacy, or a new authored story. Timing, retry and report retention are provisional balance choices. Independent audit and manual playtest are distinct from automated acceptance.

Current authored freight workers cannot be invited into the player expedition. The Released branch is verified with a controlled domain fixture, not a naturally reachable invitation for those roles. Carrier reporting follows incidental ordinary activity rather than a dedicated return journey. Naturally witnessed confirmation has not been established; the nonempty public formatting and witness capture are tested separately.
