# C5.8 - Situated diplomacy and institutional petitions

Baseline C5.7 d96e291. Rules revision 45 (SituatedDiplomacy); isolated checkpoint.c58. No authored lore, external dependencies or new inventory types.

## Negotiations
A local diplomatic accord is a voluntary reporting liaison between two living named representatives, at least one of whom is an interested member of a C5.7 association. The other may represent a different association or the existing Qinghe watch, Hall or Stonebank. Institutional roles disclose their institutional remit in these encounters. Participants must be adjacent, mutually visible, mutually familiar, nonhostile, healthy and available under existing circle safety/obligation checks. A valid, accepted, dated rumor account (confidence >=45) forms or renews the liaison. An uncommitted offer, remote relationship, or player conversation cannot create it. Pacts are personal, last 192 ticks from the last accepted exchange, and do not bind remote colleagues, transfer membership, grant faction authority or create resources. Changed affiliations are revalidated at local contact; an old pact alone cannot authorize action. Local hostility can cause a witnessed withdrawal; remote hostility or expiry does not broadcast public news.

## Local issues and reputation
A credible received report can open one diplomatic issue per recipient, linked to their actual active C5.5 inquiry and exact received account. Issues expire after 96 ticks. When views conflict, the local conversation records a dispute bound to the case ID and site. The recipient's subsequent firsthand check can match or differ from the claim; either way, the original reporter does not learn remotely. Corroboration is directional and scoped to the parties at encounter. A checked direct report may add one reputation point (max 8) for the original reporter; forwarded evidence and duplicate sample stamps do not earn points. A differing later stock observation is not proof of dishonesty and does not subtract regard or score. These are personal reputational assessments, not a globally shared faction reputation.

For 144 ticks after an actual check of a disputed case, the investigator may return newer firsthand evidence through the existing local rumor protocol. Returned evidence is preferred among otherwise eligible rumors. A response clears the precise dispute only if the receiving actor's view is no longer contested and the case ID still matches. Otherwise a counterevidence event is recorded and a new check may be necessary. Older replies cannot settle newer cases. Pacts and corroboration may influence the optional local exchange order; ordinary intra-circle exchanges retain precedence. Confidence, hop limits, reception cooldown and duplicate acceptance remain authoritative.

## Actual work and political consequences
An accepted report may motivate optional verification while the ordinary duty is Idle or Patrol. Inquiry members can check all sites; freight members only the market/depot; Hall and Stonebank representatives only the shrine/market; Qinghe watch only a shrine shortage. Existing safety, fatigue, cargo, companion, sect duty, role, cooldown and search budget constraints remain in force. Contested checks retain precedence; continuing checks and relevant diplomatic leads have preference among optional reports. Canonical effort counters distinguish chosen extended verification ticks that have a diplomatic basis and those that would not have been authorized by the individual's C5.6 or circle's C5.7 lead alone.

Qinghe's watch may carry a verified petition from a locally received shortage report. No petition is authorized until the watch's own dated check corroborates it. For 48 ticks, a qualifying petition may shorten the modern medicine contract cooldown from 96 to 48 ticks, only if the watch remains eligible, still has a verified shortage view, and the ordinary contract checks, actual need and physical perception all pass. Consumed on a real contract opening, the acceleration event links its exact existing contract ID. The existing negotiation, competing bids, purchases, deliveries, treasury, custody and fulfillment mechanisms remain the authority. No survey or relief subsystem from older revisions is re-enabled. There is no material transfer by the diplomatic system itself.

## Knowledge and persistence
Private history (192 events), active pacts/issues/replies and directional assessments are bounded by NPC count/pair count. The public journal (48 witnessed events) contains only date, participating actors, affiliation types and local negotiation/outcome category. Player Talk can record an actor's own latest dated diplomatic experience; private resolution never updates the original reporter or player remotely. Old disclosure does not become live knowledge. Canonical bytes cover all diplomacy state and replay from journaled intents. Legacy revisions and slots remain isolated.

ASCII diplomacy / diplomacynext; Ctrl+Shift+F12 pages the diplomatic journal. Ctrl+F12 circles, Ctrl+F11 profiles, Ctrl+F10 stories; F12 remains accept quest. diplomacydebug is explicitly developer-only. Read-only inspection does not advance time. EN/ID.

## Scope
Liaisons, conflicting dated claims, verified reports, and a constrained medicine-contract petition path. Not formal alliances, territorial wars, voting institutions, universal faction reputation, or pooled group economies. Third-party political effects are deliberately limited to actual local existing systems. Cross-association negotiations, returned-evidence reconciliation, disagreement, and early contract opening are covered by controlled tests; natural incidence and chosen effort are reported separately. All timing and score thresholds are provisional. Human playtest and independent audit remain separate.
