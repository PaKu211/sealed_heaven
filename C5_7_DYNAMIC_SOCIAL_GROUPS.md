# C5.7 - Dynamic social groups and collective agency

Baseline C5.6 6b381ba. Rules revision 44 (VoluntaryCircles); isolated checkpoint.c57. No authored lore or dependency changes.

## Associations and consent
A living NPC can belong to at most one association. A group begins with two physically adjacent, mutually familiar and nonhostile participants, both healthy and available for a brief negotiation. Existing companion and sect obligations, injury and fatigue restrict eligibility. Formation is an explicit deterministic negotiation, not a remote assignment. An inquiry circle requires news-sharing actors with Inquiry focus, or Vocation and at least two inquiry points; at least one has Inquiry focus. Freight associations require Partnership focus on both sides and the same latest successful project known independently by both, with no unresolved directional cooldown. The founding contract ID is private causal evidence. Maximum three members. Admission requires the sponsor (coordinator, or a local member if vacant) and all existing members physically gathered, mutually familiar, visible, eligible and interested. Groups do not merge remotely.

The first coordinator is chosen from participants by points, experience and stable actor ID, representing a local negotiated role, not authority to command. Re-election requires the entire current membership physically assembled, at least 96 ticks since election and an alternative at least two points stronger; a vacancy can be filled locally without waiting. No automatic remote successor. Departures occur independently after 48 consecutive ticks of incompatible interest, 480 ticks without personal member contact, a locally encountered hostile relationship in the leaver's own direction, or death. A departing actor has a 96-tick rejoin cooldown. One remaining member can seek a new partner; zero members dissolves the group. No forced shared regard, pooled inventory, faction authority, recruitment by omniscient location or compulsory job.

## Collective activities
Local rumor proposals between interested familiar inquiry peers receive preference in the existing rate-limited snapshot exchange. An actual received account with confidence at least 45 can create a single optional 96-tick lead for the recipient, linked to its own active inquiry ID and the actual sender. The recipient still needs personal fresh credible evidence, source trust, compatible interest and cooldown. A member with two inquiry points may choose optional verification even before its focus changes to Inquiry. Existing verification scheduler, role, fatigue, health, cargo, movement, duty, and budget constraints remain in force. No stock lookup based on group membership. Firsthand checking closes the lead, expiry or departure lapses it without proof. A check can happen during routine activity; the lead counter is not a count of dedicated investigations.

A subsequent real freight dispatch involving members of the same freight association links its project ID to that association. Material resolution is recorded via existing committed obligation hooks, even if membership changes meanwhile. It does not create extra cargo or change economy rules. Prior C5.6 individual partnership preferences remain authoritative. The association is a social structure supporting actual projects, not a separate transport or inventory engine.

## Information and persistence
Private history (192 entries) records formation, admissions, coordination, departures, lead creation/outcome, real linked dispatch/outcome and dissolution. Public witnessed negotiations retain only date, purpose and actual participants (48 entries), not private IDs, counters, remote rosters or group progress. Player Talk records only the actor's dated self-disclosed affiliation or absence of affiliation. Private departure or dissolution cannot refresh or evict public knowledge. Existing story and freight receipts remain separate. All state participates in canonical hashes; checkpoint restore replays the journal. Old versions remain distinct.

ASCII circles / circlesnext; Ctrl+F12 pages known associations in desktop UI. F12 remains accept quest, Ctrl+F11 profiles, Ctrl+F10 stories. circledebug is explicit developer-only. Read-only inspection does not advance time. EN/ID.

## Scope
Small voluntary inquiry and freight associations, not an entire political/faction, family, romance, guild economy or electoral simulation. Recruitment of a third member, leader replacement and later freight projects are verified in controlled fixtures; natural incidence is measured separately. Point thresholds, timing and capacities are provisional. Human playtest and independent audit remain separate.
