# C4.7 — Personal requests and social consequences

Baseline: C4.6 commit a271caafe8df6d2a0705b597ddb517ae83bf2084, verified Windows distribution. New rules revision 34, favors model v1, isolated checkpoint.c47. This slice adds voluntary actor-specific requests while retaining all existing NPC agency, institutional requests, physical economy, history and previous campaign revisions.

## Request creation and need

Successful ordinary face-to-face dialogue with a living, nonhostile NPC is the only opening trigger. No passive quest spawn, telepathic notification, global reputation reward, or generated NPC is added. The two need types are ordered: CareFunds first, then TravelKit. CareFunds requires a persistent wound, insufficient personal clinic fee and no available personal livelihood herb for barter. The actual fee is one coin (existing CLINIC_FEE). TravelKit requires a current companion with no packed herbs. One request per actor at a time, monotonic favor ID namespace separate from institutional requests, 180-turn exclusive deadline clamped to campaign horizon, 96-turn cooldown. No new offer at final horizon. Actor records bounded by the authored NPC population.

## Choices, resources and relationship effects

Accept or decline a known offered favor while in reach and sight of its living requester; revalidate actual need, status and relationship. Declining has no charge or hostility. Fulfil accepted travel kit through ordinary pack ACTOR (one herb transferred into existing companion inventory); ordinary provision without accepting remains available, and can close an unaccepted offer because the need has changed. Accept/fund care using fundfavor ID: transfer one actual coin from player purse into the NPC's existing personal funds, not into escrow. No direct HP or wound change, no extra material production. The NPC can subsequently pay the ordinary medic and consume physical clinic stock; funding does not guarantee treatment or override medical availability. Both fulfilments add the existing directed aid relationship effect, with actual clamped regard change in the receipt. Trust is -8..8. No bystander, faction or global reputation bonus.

A face-to-face withdrawal from a still-active accepted promise can reduce positive regard by one, never creating hostility from neutral. Remote withdrawal is an administrative personal-journal abstraction, not a delivered message; no remote relation adjustment or unseen state disclosure. If the need disappears, actor becomes hostile/dead, companionship ends, or deadline passes, close the outstanding promise without forcing the NPC schedule or charging a penalty. A successful direct part or pack commits known closure before NPC movement; unseen world changes do not update remembered records. NPC death may leave stale personal memory until the player withdraws; the existing body-evidence system remains separate.

## Memory and wire contract

One current and one remembered snapshot per known NPC; snapshots carry observed turn; terminal receipts capped at 48, persistent per-actor fulfil/withdraw counters. No duplication on repeated inspection. Only local contact or a personal withdrawal updates evidence. The normal UI renders remembered snapshots and receipts, not the developer registry or remote current relation. Actual delta, not live remote regard, is displayed. New journal tags 47/48/49/50 accept/decline/withdraw/fund with full u32 favor ID in the historical 5-byte record. Old campaigns reject them before advancing. Canonical digest binds all favors state and replay rejects tampering. Existing content packs and old checkpoint fixtures unchanged.

## Play

--c47 or --c47 --pixel. Talk (E, target T) to discover a need; invite (N) an eligible NPC, then talk again. F1 shows two recent known favors, Shift+F1 two latest receipts; Q accept, Backspace decline, Delete withdraw, period (.) fund care, O provision, R part. When an explicit adjacent target is selected, favor actions select only its eligible request; otherwise require a unique matching favor. CLI: favors [count], favorlog [count], acceptfavor [ID], declinefavor [ID], withdrawfavor [ID], fundfavor [ID], pack ACTOR. Ambiguous selections need a known ID. favordebug developer only. Old controls retained. EN and ID supported. Save slot checkpoint.c47, no automatic migration.

## Evidence and limits

Natural seed-0 Road outrider trace: gather, talk, recruit, talk, offer at turn 30; accept at 31 and provide at 32. Five canonical branches: fulfil, decline, withdraw, part, unpromised pack. Trust delta +1 for fulfilment. Core tests also use explicit wounded fixtures to verify actual transfer, paid clinic treatment, insufficient funds, other resources, caps, deadline and stale knowledge. Six passive seeds x 2000 turns compare C4.6 public state and ledgers; four mixed seeds x 1000 turns check deterministic twins. No wounded NPC emerged in those passive runs; natural playable successful care-funding journey is not established. This is not universal interpersonal quest generation, debt lending, faction reputation propagation, or full lore integration. Separate Windows automated verification, not human playtest or independent audit. Design choices: need priority, one herb/fee, 180/96 durations, 48 receipts, modest local relationship deltas. No new dependencies or remote Git push.
