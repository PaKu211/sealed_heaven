# C5.5 - Emergent causal narratives

Baseline C5.4 449f4c2. Rules revision 42. Separate checkpoint.c55. No new dependencies, authored lore, or change to material simulation rules.

## Causal project episodes
Every actual coordinated dispatch opens one canonical episode keyed by the existing project ID. The material resolution is attached only by the obligation close hook. The optional handoff and final direct/relayed acknowledgement, register reading, or unreported termination attach only to the same project. A carrier loss terminates without testimony; unreported outcomes do not invent a speaker or location. Active episodes survive bounded underlying event histories. Completed world project archive: 96. Engine-only career counters record participants' projects, outcomes and acknowledgement status. These counters are descriptive and never authorize goods, change regard, or choose NPC goals.

The separate player projection learns from witnessed dispatches, existing witnessed delivery evidence, locally read manifests and witnessed verbal exchanges. A report heard without its prior dispatch does not reconstruct hidden event time, location, quantities or opening. A register gives only its actual recorded delivery and acquisition time, not the grower's private reaction. An unseen closure never updates or evicts a public episode. Public project cap 48, eviction by oldest acquisition then ID. Re-reading the same receipt does not update recency. An unresolved known dispatch may suggest consulting the destination register; it does not assert that a manifest exists or a project remains active.

## Inquiry episodes
An actual received resource account that leaves the actor uncertain opens an episode for that actor and site. At most one active inquiry per actor/site. First account retains origin, via, hop, original date and acquisition date. First competing dated accounts are retained as evidence of uncertainty, not proof of a lie or causal responsibility. The ordinary observation barrier can close an inquiry with a dated firsthand check. Expiry, loss of relevance or death terminates without asserting proof. Repeat updates extend the existing uncertain episode rather than invent a new quest. Actor-local IDs prevent leakage of private episode counts. Global completed archive cap 128; independent player completed archive cap 48. NPC career counters include inquiry totals and closed statuses.

A player inquiry has an optional lead to visit the known site while uncertainty persists. No scripted quest, forced narrative, rewards, or automatic movement. There is no claim that the older reports caused economic changes, or that they represent separate independent sources. Local checks can occur during routine work, not only intentional investigation. The actor's dated evidence remains historical after it expires, with an explicit lapse or check ending.

## Determinism, controls, and scope
All episode and career state participates in canonical hashing. Save/load reconstructs from the canonical journal. Old rules and save slots stay unchanged. ASCII stories lists only player-known episodes; storiesnext pages them. Ctrl+F10 cycles the narrative pages in the desktop client; F10 retains road history. storydebug requires explicit developer mode. EN/ID. Queries never advance time.
The first two episode domains are material freight commitments and resource inquiries. This is an observational causal-memory foundation and optional opportunity layer, not a generalized narrative director, quest generator, LLM writer, or complete lifetime simulation. Human playtest and independent audit remain separate.
