# C5.3 - Situated communication and information agency

Baseline C5.2 e257d7e250477695cac704b5544fd750bc59e160. Rules revision 40, isolated checkpoint.c53. No dependency or authored lore changes.

## Physical reports
A completed or interrupted C5.2 project that produces a pending resolution opens a single message with the original carrier, addressed contributor, project ID and original resolution. The existing obligation is authoritative; there is no invented result or extra inventory. Dead-carrier resolution does not create testimony. Healthy empty-handed messengers without companion or sect obligations may choose CarryMessage at priority 45 (50 for disruption or age >= 360 ticks). This is below medical, combat, institutional and fatigue priorities. The exact scheduler bypasses its routine certificate for exceptional message choices. Existing work and goods are not consumed by messaging. Each chosen search uses ordinary movement and fatigue, never teleportation.

The messenger uses the recipient's current tile only within ordinary perception. Otherwise it searches the known dispatch hub, producer workplace, and authored home, in a deterministic circuit. Two local waiting ticks change site. At most 64 chosen search ticks before a 24-tick yield to other work; an exhausted messenger may camp instead of abandoning a journey to walk home, but off-duty obligations remain. These are provisional tuning parameters. Existing report lifetime 480 ticks remains authoritative. No remote target selection based on hidden current location or activity.

## Optional delegation
After the original messenger has used a full search budget and is yielding, it may physically meet an eligible idle/patrolling, news-sharing acquaintance who knows the recipient. Mutual nonhostile familiar relations are required for handoff; acceptance requires the recipient's own trust in the messenger at actual contact. One holder and at most one handoff, no second relay or transitive increase in trust. The origin retains its first-hand knowledge and may still report incidentally. A local register or direct/relayed confirmation removes the pending message exactly once. A failed relay cannot silently teleport back to its origin. The communication history identifies the actual messenger and original source; successful fulfilment still affects the original pair, never the intermediary's regard automatically. If no report is received, no remote blame. The preexisting C5.2 recipient-death/expiry cleanup stays in force.

## Knowledge and interface
Only physical witnessed handoffs and verbal receipts enter the public message journal; no private search target, trust delta, register reaction or remote failure is exposed. Existing teams and obligations journals remain separate, with direct acknowledgements represented as before. Relay acknowledgements are in the message journal to preserve their distinct source. All message state, bounded history (128) and observed history (48) participate in canonical hashing and replay. The previous revisions retain identical state encoding.

## Scope and acceptance
The first message type is the real freight commitment outcome. Existing incident, regional and institutional news remain in their existing systems; not a general rumor/chat network. Delegation is intentionally conservative; natural incidence is evaluated separately from controlled tests. Human playtest and external audit remain distinct. Results are recorded in the acceptance evidence, not implied by this design specification.
