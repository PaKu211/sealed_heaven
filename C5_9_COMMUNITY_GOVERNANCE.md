Baseline C5.8 2ba3608e60214d43008c51fbf90f02810ad36f56. Revision 46 (CommunityGovernance), isolated checkpoint.c59. Implements the reviewed R2 candidate on PC15 and subsequent no-award review refinement. No new dependencies or authored lore.

## Physical institutional records
Qinghe medicine office and Reed Hall offerings office each keep a durable board and bounded history. The current living official must be within reach and line of sight of the institution, healthy, available, not a companion or engaged in an active sect duty; Hall review occurs during daytime. Readings are at least 16 ticks apart; a gap greater than 96 resets consecutive evidence. Two low readings establish reserve priority, two adequate readings restore routine, intermediate stock breaks the streak. Readings are snapshots, not continuous observation.
- Qinghe low <=2 and below shrine target; adequate >= target. Reserve can authorize stock below target after tick 33.
- Hall low = 0; adequate >=2. Reserve can authorize an empty offerings contract after tick 64 even without the ordinary rivalry-pressure trigger.
- All thresholds are provisional balancing choices.

## Consequences and boundaries
The existing contract engine remains the only procurement authority. Single active need, ordinary cooldown, physical requester observation, C5.8 verified petition exception, bidding, suspensions, treasury, purchase, custody and fulfillment remain authoritative. Optional reserve orders require current local stock revalidation and available office authority. The ledger links actual opened contract IDs. Hall stock restoration can resolve unawarded contracts before award or procurement when the requester perceives the Hall; it cannot cancel already committed provider cargo. The office ledger issues no goods or coins.

## Accountability and succession
Only committed local inspections of actual awarded receipts file delivered or unfulfilled audits. The latest inspection and highest case ID with an inspected outcome are separate. A failure of the highest inspected case delays optional reserve orders for 48 ticks after inspection; ordinary orders are unaffected. Late old receipts cannot replace newer known outcomes. The institutional record persists independently of personal regard. New Hall orders use the chartered current leader. Old requester/provider IDs never change. A charter-recognized successor may inspect predecessor's uninspected Hall receipts only at the Hall and when eligible; personal regard and information history are not inherited. A new leader becomes a preferred institutional testimony recipient only at physical contact, while prior known routes remain. Existing testimony/dispute authority checks recognize the current office holder. The successor must actually reach the office to use the record.

Review refinement: a real no-award decision after solicitation or a local pre-award restoration can enter a bounded private queue owned by its requester. No mechanical distant expiry, death or carrier failure is recorded as a personally known negotiation. Only that official, back at the office and review-eligible, files it with actual event and filing ticks. No-award is distinct from unfulfilled awarded work; restored local stock is distinct from failure or delivery. After filing a no-award, optional reserve orders are deferred until 192 ticks after the actual decision; ordinary needs retain their existing policy. A later return after the deadline incurs no extra wait. Pending facts not filed before succession are not magically learned by a successor. Pending and public histories each cap at 48 per office. Counters persist. The record is not a complete ledger of every failed, expired or externally withdrawn contract.

## Player agency and journal
Successful shrine deliveries including sect duty delivery, Hall enrollment offerings and Hall patronage offerings count as actual deposits. Rejected transfers do not count. A deposit can make a new reserve order unnecessary or close an unawarded local need; there is no free stock or loyalty buff. Only within reach and line of sight of the site, at an observation barrier, does the player read an updated board. Snapshots retain read and review ticks, policy evidence, order and audited outcome totals, filed negotiation outcomes and caution deadline, and contribution count. Remote change cannot modify a previously read board. All private and public state is canonical.
