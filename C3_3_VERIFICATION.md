# C3.3 — Persistent tactical wounds (revision 23)

Based on C3.2 `38e2ad4`. Default ASCII writes `checkpoint.c33`; `--c32` retains revision 22. Earlier campaign digests and the frozen C0.1 kernel remain unchanged. GDD direction remains a dense, authored slice, not a claim of C4.0 completion.

After defenses and wards, a nonfatal blow of at least two damage to a body at half HP or lower leaves a persistent wound: a hard blow (power three or more) injures arms; other blows injure legs. Wounds accumulate through subsequent survivable blows, including after HP-only healing. Severe arm wounds reduce player and NPC strikes by one; severe leg wounds stop player movement, NPC travel/retreat, and courtesy swaps on alternating turns. They also make Qi circulation unsafe. NPCs with severe injuries withdraw instead of retaliating, then travel to a staffed clinic if an acceptable medic and shrine stock exist. Each `treatwounds` or NPC wound-care action consumes one physical shrine herb into the existing consumed ledger, heals one wound and restores up to one HP; an absent/refusing medic or empty stock cannot provide it. Existing HP remedies do **not** erase wounds. Wound events retain actor, site and whether the player actually witnessed them; `wounds` and `woundlog` expose personal state and witnessed events, not remote omniscience.

Public seed-zero route pursues a moving clinic medic by movement and two actual `attack` intents, causes a witnessed leg wound, and restores the resulting checkpoint exactly. The same complete ASCII route (32 ticks) passed save/load and is captured in `/tmp/opencode/c33-ascii-play.txt`. A separate simulation test makes an NPC walk to the staffed clinic and take two real medicine-consuming care actions; unrelated autonomous medicine consumption is deliberately **not** counted as this patient's private bill. Focused fixture tests isolate two nonfatal bouts with intervening HP care, limb penalties, NPC retreat, exact two-actor medicine transfer, ward mitigation, distant event privacy and revision-22 isolation; these fixture-mutated states are **not** claimed as replayable saves. Four seeds run 180 public turns, checking replay at four milestones and monotonic wound IDs. Earlier C3.2 multi-seed herb conservation tests remain in the full suite.

Offline Linux evidence: root `cargo build --offline` and `cargo test --offline` (**308 passed, 1 ignored**, 2 doctests passed); playable `--no-default-features` build, **60 library tests** and all integration suites passed. Root and playable strict all-target Clippy (`-D warnings`), Rustfmt checks and whitespace checks passed. No Windows verification, human playtest or performance benchmark.

Still alpha: wounds are a small two-limb tactical model, not systemic anatomy or long-term disability; severe injuries may remain untreated during hostility, poor staffing or resource shortage. Future work should test richer recovery choices and independent agent livelihoods rather than calling this C4.0.
