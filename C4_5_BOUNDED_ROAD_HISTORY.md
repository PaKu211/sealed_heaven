## Encounters and consequences

Cache marker ! is nonblocking. Player visibility is authoritative; no camera action creates knowledge. At the initialization and player/NPC commit boundaries, record first observed tick. The player can recover the entire remaining physical deposit into their pack or entrust it to its actual route carrier, Bao Jun (west) or Li Wen (east). Both player actions need reach <=1 and sight of the cache; entrust additionally requires a living full-HP empty-handed carrier within reach and sight of both the cache and player, who does not refuse aid. Successful entrust transfers to the carrier's inventory and increments real regard via the ordinary aid relation. A westbound reserve satisfies up to that many outstanding requested herbs. No teleportation, guarantee of future delivery, free reward or new NPC. Rejected domain actions can consume the existing canonical turn but cannot perform a cache transfer.

A living eligible carrier within reach may choose the local cache as its ordinary livelihood freight candidate. All higher-priority needs, obligations, fatigue and care still use the existing planner. The actual ID-ordered commit revalidates recipient, position, health, cargo, stock and goal. A pickup uses the normal freight work action for that turn, replacing (not appending to) movement/stock loading. Subsequent freight uses existing physical cargo, travel, and delivery. A preempted/dead carrier cannot collect. The first successful recovery resolves a cache forever; no respawn or duplicate claim. Deposits may remain untouched for an entire campaign. Fixed two-entry record, including recipient, method, tick and direct-witness flag; no unbounded event retention.

## Player knowledge and controls

ASCII: history (discovered immutable deposits and directly witnessed outcomes), encounters (currently visible cache stock and options), recovercache [ID], entrustcache [ID]. Without ID, the latter two select the first locally available eligible cache; malformed/no candidate is a read-only UI notice. An explicit numeric ID is admitted and revalidated in the domain. Developer-only historylog exposes complete records. Seeing an already empty cache never reveals the identity/time/method of a remote resolution. A witnessed resolution is remembered after leaving; no live remote stock query is added.

Pixel: F6 visible cache, F7 recover, F8 entrust, F10 history. F2/F3/F4, F5 save and F9 load retained. ! is a distinct sprite; actor/corpse takes precedence at an occupied marker. EN/ID locale pack adds nineteen keys. All projections and inspection are read-only.

New --c45 launch and checkpoint.c45. Intent tags 42 and 43 use the full u32 cache ID and are admitted only in revision 32; historic 5-byte records unchanged. Outcome tags 48/49 encode the actual transfer. Save restore reconstructs from seed and admitted journal; scenario/state digests prevent relabeling, fabricated discovery or witness state. Older slots remain isolated, no automatic migration.
