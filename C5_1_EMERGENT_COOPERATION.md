# C5.1 - Emergent cooperation and collective agency

Baseline C5.0 644f1cdf939bc8f448f95bc11db909130c63f682. Rules revision 38, isolated checkpoint.c51. No new dependencies, authored lore or automatic migration.

## Material collective projects

Two complementary public logistics pairs are supported: Willowford grower + Qinghe courier, and Pineford grower + Willowford carrier. Both actors must actually be within reach of the shared hub, see one another, be healthy/unwounded, below fatigue threshold, without companion/sect obligation or personal livelihood stock, and mutually nonhostile. A paired dispatch is an optional coordination of their existing snapshot-selected routine work. The contributor must already be carrying public freight and plan to stock the hub; the empty carrier must independently plan collection or an eligible routine idle. No urgent/medical/combat/institutional task, road-cache pickup, or existing project is preempted. Frontier demand comes only from the carrier's own fresh market receipt. Qinghe uses its local published order remaining.

Two existing work actions commit grower-before-carrier, one fatigue increment and traffic commit per actor, before remaining normal commits. Existing physical stock/load/dispatch functions enforce reserve, order quota, capacity and local source. A positive portion of freshly deposited stock is allocated to the carrier after protecting reserve; herbs are fungible, not unique item identities. No new inventory or resource generation. An idle carrier can collect now rather than miss an available shipment. No free trust, gratitude, compensation or new relationship score.

A project records participants, route, opening/deadline, fresh allocation and total physical shipment. At most one active project per participant. Ordinary movement and higher-priority choices continue. Arrival is accepted only by the original destination unloading handler with the exact shipment amount before expiry. Changed cargo, carrier death, joining a player expedition or 240-tick deadline close the project without changing physical goods or creating automatic blame. This is a narrow freight collaboration implementation; no autonomous conspiracy, deliberate betrayal, general faction projects or new quest system.

## Situated knowledge

A player may directly witness dispatch, and link arrival to it only if the opening was witnessed. A successful delivery additionally posts a public shipping receipt at its physical destination. A living player who comes within reach and line of sight of that site reads the receipt on simulation advancement; the recorded source distinguishes direct observation from the posted receipt. No private group state or remote tracking. Local register cap 16 per destination; known journal 48; internal event history 96. Private failures do not enter the player's journal. Inspection does not advance time. Canonical state includes all records, known evidence and read cursors. Earlier revisions retain their exact checkpoint semantics. Content identity lookup corrected to recognize supported revisions 36-38 and reject unknown revisions.
