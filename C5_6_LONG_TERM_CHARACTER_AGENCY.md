# C5.6 - Long-term character agency and personal pursuits

Baseline C5.5 03dae49. Rules revision 43, isolated checkpoint.c56. No authored lore or dependency changes. Existing character agency vocation/ambition, martial training, cultivation, economy, relationships, scheduler and higher-priority goals remain authoritative.

## Learning and focus

Each authored NPC has a durable personal profile, bounded inquiry and partnership points (0-8), and descriptive counters. Focus begins at Vocation and may shift to Inquiry or Partnership at three points. A currently eligible focus persists until the alternative is at least two points higher; if a focus is no longer eligible the best eligible alternative wins. Ties outside a current eligible focus prefer Inquiry. A focus is a secondary pursuit, not a profession change, guaranteed outcome or global story goal. No point creates inventory, power, reputation or XP in the martial system.

An actual C5.5 inquiry completed through the actor's own dated local observation contributes one point (two if the actor committed verification effort for that same episode). Each site grants points at most once per 32 ticks. Routine visits still count as checks, not necessarily deliberate investigations. A lapsed inquiry with committed effort reduces inquiry points by one and pauses learned optional verification for 48 ticks; mere expiration without effort is not personal failure. Dead actors do not learn. Effort uses the actual actor/site/episode ID and is removed at closure. A successful extended check yields for at least 24 ticks before the next learned optional investigation.

The trained Inquiry focus can propose a site with a credible dated reported availability band (confidence >=45, source known and nonhostile), even without contradictory accounts. Existing contested verification retains precedence and can still run during the optional learned pause. Extended work prefers a previously attempted eligible site, then scarcity, confidence and stable site order. All old health, fatigue, cargo, companion, sect duty, role/duty and search limits remain in force. The ordinary travel/observation system supplies proof. Both contested and learned reported work keep the 48 chosen-tick per-site search budget and 48-tick yield. Existing scheduler certificate checks the same proposal.

## Personally known projects and relationships

The carrier learns a material outcome from own completed/changed/withdrawn/expired cargo commitment while alive. The contributor learns only at actual direct/relayed acknowledgement or local matching manifest reading. A lost carrier is not a speaker; an unreported outcome does not teach the distant partner. Each directed partner memory deduplicates project IDs, records acquisition time and known successes/disruptions. Successful known project adds three partnership points, a voluntary release subtracts one, changed/expired cargo subtracts two. A known disruption pauses optional coordinated dispatch with that specific partner for 96 ticks (48 for voluntary release); normal trade and pre-existing cooperation checks are unchanged. Directional learning does not itself change regard or establish blame. Partnership focus may add one or two points to existing valid commitment delivery/message scores depending on familiarity and nonhostility; message score capped at 51. No new obligation, hidden remote position or health override.

## Memory, interface and replay

The engine stores private profile/effort/partner state and 192 recent evidence-linked development events, including before/after focus and original project or inquiry ID. Public knowledge is independent: a successful ordinary conversation with a living visible nonhostile NPC can record its voluntarily shared current focus as a dated snapshot. No private points, history, partner assessments, or live updates to old disclosures. Known rows are bounded by authored NPC count. No public learning from witnessing a project alone. ASCII profiles, profilesnext; Ctrl+F11 pages the last shared pursuits in pixel UI. F11 remains institutional quests; Ctrl+F10 stories remains available. profiledebug is explicit developer-only. Read-only queries do not advance time. EN/ID. All development state is in canonical hashes, replayed from intents; previous revisions and save slots remain isolated.

## Scope and balancing

This extends the two existing C5.5 domains. It is not a complete lifetime progression, career switch, skill-tree, romance, generational or faction-rank system. Point thresholds, credibility, cooldowns and history capacities are provisional design choices. The initial natural probe motivated permitting both scarce and adequate credible leads, preferring ongoing work and adding a success yield; natural frequency and effects are measured separately. Human playtest and independent audit remain separate.
